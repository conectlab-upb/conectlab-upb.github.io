About the lab

One place, two kinds of connections

The name says it all: in EB104 we connect equipment to equipment and people to people. Every result becomes learning material for the next generation.

We connect equipment

Real devices that talk to each other: locally, in the cloud or both, over the right protocol.

  • IIoT: switchboards, power analyzers, meters, inverters (Modbus, MQTT, ESPHome)
  • IoMT: medical monitoring sensors and devices
  • Mobile robots with ROS
  • SCADA dashboards in Home Assistant
We connect people

Students work in teams on real equipment, from the first lab to the thesis.

  • Shared step-by-step modules used by several courses
  • Bachelor’s, master’s and PhD theses grown from real projects
  • Student teams with their own Home Assistant instances
  • Collaboration with other labs, clinics and industry

Fields

Two main directions

IoT applied where it matters: in electrical installations and in health monitoring.

IIoT for electrical engineeringIIoT

The Industrial Internet of Things applied to electrical installations: data acquisition from switchboards, power analyzers, meters and inverters over Modbus RTU/TCP and MQTT, with ESP32 gateways and Home Assistant. The data goes where it is useful: locally or to the cloud.

ModbusMQTTESPHomeSCADA

IoMT for medical monitoringIoMT

The Internet of Medical Things: sensors and devices that collect and transmit health data, locally or to the cloud. In the lab, medical monitoring is done for teaching and research, with a focus on data acquisition and data security.

biomedical sensorse-Healthdata security

Energy and power quality

Consumption, photovoltaics and power quality as an application field.

Robotics with ROS

Mobile robots, sensors and communication as a project platform.
